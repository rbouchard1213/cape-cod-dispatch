You’re on the future-trades list

Pretend version: nothing was sent. On the real board, here is exactly what happens after an application arrives:

  1. The application goes into the Busy Bees future-trades waitlist, under its trade and towns.
  2. The back-office AI sorts it, checks for missing lines or a duplicate, and keeps the count for that trade and area.
  3. Busy Bees reviews it personally and decides whether it is approved for the waitlist.
  4. When a trade reaches three approved contractors in an area, the owner can decide whether to design and open it. The jobs, sizes, and fees are built first — the trade is never switched on automatically.

No customer requests are taken for a trade until the owner opens it. If that happens, the contractors on its waitlist are contacted first.
