# Filling the Roster — Contractor Recruiting Pack

Roster target: 6–10 contractors. Two per service across the 8 towns beats ten in one town.
Priority order: (1) tree guy, (2) storm/cleanup crew, (3) hauling guy, (4) power-washing/fall-cleanup help.

How joining works (for now): they text you at (413) 234-6351 with business name,
services, towns, and the cell that should get job texts. You forward it to Muse —
they're on the Mission Control roster in about a minute.

---

## 1) TEXT / DM SCRIPT — for guys you already know

Hey [name] — it's Bobby. I just launched a local dispatch service for the Cape —
homeowners send one request, and the job gets texted to a contractor who covers
that town. I'm building the roster now and thought of you for [tree work / hauling /
cleanups].

No subscription, no monthly anything. You only pay a small lead fee when a job
comes to you — $35 tree, $30 storm, $25 haul/wash/fall — and only for jobs in your
towns. You quote the customer yourself and keep the whole job price.

Want in? Text me your business name, what you cover, and your towns and I'll put
you on the list. First leads go to the first guys on the roster.

## Where to find them

- **Your warm circle first:** your friend (roster #2), Greg's crew and the guys
  Greg subs to, anyone you've crossed paths with on jobs.
- **Facebook:** Cape Cod trade/contractor groups, town groups, and guys already
  advertising tree/firewood/hauling services on Marketplace — they're already
  hunting for work.
- **In person:** landscape supply yards, lumber yards, saw shops, early-morning
  coffee stops — where crews start their day.
- **Craigslist (Cape Cod):** the services + gigs sections — plenty of solo guys
  looking for exactly this.

## Roster rules worth saying out loud

- Fees are per lead you accept — never a subscription.
- First on the roster in a town gets first shot at that town's leads.
- They must actually text back fast — homeowners get routed to the next
  contractor if there's no reply (keeps the service's name good).
