Part 1 of the review packet — read first, then reply "I agree." This is the full agreement text, the same words as the paper packet.

Between: the owner of Busy Bees ("Busy Bees")
And: the reviewer named below ("Reviewer")
Date: the date the Reviewer agrees (recorded with their review code)

Busy Bees is not public yet. Busy Bees is letting the Reviewer see a private preview of the new Busy Bees website ("the Preview") so they can test it and give feedback. In exchange for access and the review pay, the Reviewer agrees to all of the following:

1. Keep it private

  • Do not share the review link, the preview link, or the password-free web addresses with anyone.
  • Do not post about the Preview publicly — no posts, stories, or comments describing it — until Busy Bees announces it publicly.
  • Do not show screenshots or screen recordings to anyone except Busy Bees. (Sending your finished notes, checklist, and problem-spot screenshots back to Busy Bees is expected and allowed.)

2. Do not copy it

  • The Preview — its design, layout, wording, prices, fees, service lists, and the way the business works — belongs to Busy Bees.
  • The Reviewer will not copy, rebuild, imitate, or use any part of it, for themselves or for anyone else, during the review or after it. This includes building or helping build a similar dispatch or lead service based on it.

3. Feedback

  • Everything the Reviewer sends Busy Bees (notes, checklists, screenshots, ideas, suggestions) goes to Busy Bees only, and Busy Bees may use it freely, in any way, forever, without owing anything beyond the review pay below.

4. The pay

  • Standard: $30 cash or a $35 gift card for a completed review with notes returned by Friday, November 13, 2026 (+$10 if notes are back by Friday, November 6). Real extra finds pay $5 each (up to $25 per person); one truly exceptional catch pays $20. Busy Bees decides what counts as a real find; the same find reported twice counts once.
  • Contractors: instead of the cash or gift card, a contractor's review pay is one free month of Busy Bees membership (a $49 value). If membership is already free for them at the time, the month waits until paid membership starts.
  • Pay is made in full, all at once, when the finished notes are checked in — never in pieces.
  • Family reviewers (Renea, Kim): the separate family terms sent to them personally apply instead of the standard amounts where they differ.

5. If this agreement is broken

  • Breaking Section 1 or Section 2 causes real harm that is hard to measure exactly. The Reviewer agrees that if they break Section 1 or Section 2, they will owe Busy Bees $5,000 — a fair, agreed estimate of the value of the site, its design, and the work in it — and Busy Bees may also pursue any further real damages and legal remedies the law allows.

6. How agreeing works

  • The Reviewer agrees by replying "I agree" to the Busy Bees message containing these terms, and/or by tapping "I agree" on the review page pop-up after typing their name and review code. The name, code, and date are recorded with the Busy Bees reviewer tracker.
  • The Reviewer's personal review code (example: BEE-01) ties their agreement, their notes, and their pay together.
