Straight answers before you ask

Is it really free to request?

Yes. Submitting is always free and no card is ever asked for. You pay only the contractor directly — and only for work you approve.

Who actually shows up to do the work?

A local contractor who covers your town. They'll text you first with who they are and a quote — nothing starts until you say yes to it.

How fast will I hear back?

Busy Bees reviews each request and replies by text with the next step. Timing depends on which local contractors are available, so there is no guaranteed response time.

How do I pay for the work itself?

Directly to your contractor when the job's done, the way you've always paid local pros. If a card payment is ever involved, it runs through Stripe's secure checkout — your card details are never stored on this site or shared.

What happens to my phone number?

Busy Bees uses it for text updates about your job. It is never sold or put on a call list, and we never voice-call you. Questions in the meantime? Give us a buzz — text us at (508) 258-9899.
