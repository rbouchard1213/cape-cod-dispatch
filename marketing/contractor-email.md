## Sending notes (why we do NOT mass-blast)

- A bulk blast from a personal Gmail is exactly what spam filters eat — and it can
  get the Gmail account restricted, which would kill the business inbox.
- What works: individual emails, one business at a time, with the [brackets]
  filled in. 10–15 a day, spread out, reads like a real person wrote it —
  because one did.
- Only email businesses that publish an email address publicly for contact.
- Anyone who replies "no thanks" (or anything like it) gets removed permanently,
  no follow-ups.
- Bobby approves the final list and the first batch before anything sends.
