Levels & verified

Two separate things: your level comes from jobs you've done. Verified comes from documents you choose to show. Neither can be bought.

Levels — how the line works

  • Level 1 — 0–4 jobs done
  • Level 2 — 5–19 jobs done
  • Level 3 — 20–49 jobs done
  • Level 4 — 50+ jobs done

New jobs are offered to verified contractors first, and higher levels get a longer head start before the job moves down the line:

  • Level 4 gets 60 minutes · Level 3 gets 45 minutes
  • Level 2 gets 30 minutes · Level 1 gets 15 minutes
  • No job waits more than 1 hour total before it cascades.

Your standing grows with good work and the private ratings customers leave after jobs. Declining a job never hurts you — a pass is a pass, never a strike.

Verified — show a licence and/or insurance

Adding a photo of your licence and/or insurance is optional. If you show them:

  • Insurance shown = 5% off your fees.
  • Licence + insurance = 10% off your fees.
  • Licence alone = 0% until insurance is also shown.
  • Verified unlocks priority first-look at new jobs, and standing-accept: if you opt in, jobs under $35 that match your work are accepted for you automatically.

Plain and simple: Busy Bees does NOT run background checks. Verified only means you showed us those documents — nothing more is checked or promised.
