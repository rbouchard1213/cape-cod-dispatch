Job: Tree removal

Flat fee if you accept: $30

The fee you see is the fee you pay — it never changes after you accept. For repeat work, the fee is paid once per arrangement, never per visit. One arrangement = one customer, one address, one kind of work; a new season or a different kind of work is a new offer with a new fee. A plan cancelled more than 24 hours after you accept earns no credit, before or after the first visit. An arrangement lasts at most 12 months; when it ends, the customer chooses whether to continue with the same contractor under a new arrangement and fee, or send the request to the board.

If the job dies through no fault of yours — bad number, fake or duplicate lead, a customer who answers nobody within 4 days of you accepting, a cancel within 24 hours of you accepting, or a no-show at the door — the fee comes back as credit on your account.

One flat fee, only if you accept. No commission — you keep 100% of the job price you quote.
