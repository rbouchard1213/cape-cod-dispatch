Contractor side · Future trades — not live

Don’t see your trade?

Busy Bees is starting with the services already on the board. This page is only an application process for future trades — it does not make a new trade live.

What this is — and what it isn’t

  • It is: a waitlist for trades such as auto repair, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, or appliance repair.
  • It isn’t: a customer service, a promise of jobs, or a way to start receiving offers now.
  • There is no fee to apply, and applying does not join you to the live roster for a trade that has not opened.

How the process works

  1. You apply by text with the short template on the next screen.
  2. Busy Bees reviews every application personally. Busy Bees’ back-office AI can sort applications by trade and town, spot missing information or duplicates, count the list, and draft possible jobs and sizes for the owner to review.
  3. The AI never approves an application, never adds a trade, never promises work, and never contacts you on its own. The owner decides.
  4. A new trade is normally considered when three approved contractors offer the same trade in the same area. Before it can open, the owner still has to approve its jobs, size questions, flat fees, and any legal or licensing requirements.
  5. Until the owner opens the trade, Busy Bees takes no customer requests for it.
