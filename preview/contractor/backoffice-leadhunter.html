Lead Hunter

A paid extra for members, still being built. This page is a first look — it is not on sale, and there's no price on it yet. When it opens, members will hear first.

What it will do

The board already sees more local demand than it can fill — requests in towns with no contractor yet, jobs that got turned away, people asking around in local groups and search. Lead Hunter will forage those signals for you — the way bees range out and bring back what the hive needs, it works that demand and brings back the jobs that check out:

  • It watches for local people saying they need work done — on the board, in public local posts, in local search.
  • It reaches out, confirms it's a real homeowner with a real job, and books the conversation — or the visit — for you.
  • A lead it brings you is yours alone. It's never handed to a second contractor, never resold, and never mixed into the board's regular offers.
  • You only ever hear about the ones that check out — real person, real job, local.

Why it's a member extra

Finding and chasing work takes hours a week. Lead Hunter is Busy Bees doing that legwork as an optional paid bonus for members — separate from board jobs, which keep working exactly the way they do now, fee-only-on-accept and all. It will never change who gets board offers: hunting is a service on top, not a louder voice in the line.

Want first word?

Tell us and we'll let you know the moment Lead Hunter opens for members — what it costs, what counts as a lead, all of it in plain words before anyone's asked for a cent.
