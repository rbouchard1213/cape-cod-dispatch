## Posting notes

- **Groups to use:** Mid Cape Cod Virtual Yard Sale, Yarmouth/town community groups, Cape Cod yard sale & community boards. Read each group's rules first — some limit business posts to certain days; follow them so posts don't get removed.
- **Best times:** weekday evenings 6–9 PM, and the first morning after any storm.
- **Always attach the flyer image** and keep the link in the post.
- **Reply to every comment fast** — comments push the post up the feed, and a quick "just sent you a text/PM" builds trust in public.
- **Don't spam:** one group post per group per week is plenty; rotate launch → fall → storm posts.
- Google Business Profile + Nextdoor business page are the next free listings to claim (30 min each, no street address shown — service area only).
