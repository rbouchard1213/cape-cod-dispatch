Keep your phone buzzing.

Busy Bees is one board where local customers ask for yard, home, and hauling help — and the requests come to contractors like you, by text.

The deal, in plain words

  • You only pay when you accept a job — one flat fee, shown in the offer before you decide.
  • No subscription to join. No commission. You keep 100% of the job price — you quote the customer yourself.
  • The most any fee ever is is $100.
  • Declining is always free, and passing on a job never counts against you.
  • Customers never pay anything. The fee is yours, only on jobs you accept.

How it works

  1. Get the text. A matched job arrives by text: the job, the town, the size, and the flat lead fee — all in plain words.
  2. Confirm and pay. Say yes, pay the flat fee through a secure checkout, and only then are the customer's details released to you. Declining is always free.
  3. Do the job. You talk to the customer through the board's private relay — neither phone number is ever shown. You quote the job price yourself, on site.

Good to know

  • Verified discount: show insurance = 5% off the fee · licence + insurance = 10% off · licence alone = 0% until insurance is also shown. One discount per lead — discounts never combine.
  • Welcome pick (choose one when you join): 50% off your first lead, or 25% off your first 3 leads. Nothing is ever free — discounts are percentages only.
  • Referrals: bring a new contractor and you both get 25% off a lead once they're approved. Bring a new customer and you get 20% off your next lead when their first request is accepted.

Optional extra — your own booking page

Membership is never required for board jobs, and the deal above never changes. Members also get their own Busy Bees page to hand to customers they already have; bookings that come through that page cost membership plus just 10% of the normal fee instead of the full fee.

Membership: $19/week · $49/month · $490/year. Membership never buys ranking, ratings, level, or verified status — ever.
