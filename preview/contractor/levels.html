Levels & verified

Two separate things: your level comes from jobs you've done. Verified comes from documents you choose to show. Neither can be bought — membership never buys ranking, ratings, level, or verified status.

Levels — how the line works

  • Level 1 — 0–4 jobs done
  • Level 2 — 5–19 jobs done
  • Level 3 — 20–49 jobs done
  • Level 4 — 50+ jobs done

New jobs start at the top of the ladder: every contractor at the highest level with a match for the job gets it. Verified contractors at that level get the first look. The clock never runs on a level with no matching contractor. The job opens in steps (decided Oct 31, 2026): Level 4 sees it first, Level 3 joins at 15 minutes, Level 2 at 30 minutes, and Level 1 at 45 minutes — from then it's open to the whole roster, and the first contractor to accept wins. The clock is capped, so no job ever waits more than 1 hour total before it lands in the Busy Bees dispatch queue.

Your level grows with jobs done. Separately, the private ratings customers leave feed your trust score — figured from jobs you complete through the board (70%) and your ratings (30%) — it grows only from completed jobs and ratings, and paying for anything adds nothing to it. Your trust score is kept private, and membership can't change it. Declining a job is never a strike against you — a pass is a pass. Not answering counts the same as declining: free, and never a strike.

Verified — show your business paperwork

Showing your business paperwork is optional — any business paper counts: a trade license, a town business certificate, or insurance. We keep a copy on file — we don't check it with the issuer. No paperwork is needed to join the board or get jobs; this is only the bonus. If you show them:

  • Insurance shown = 5% off your fees.
  • Insurance + a second paper (a license or business certificate — it has to be a different kind; two papers of the same kind count as one) = 10% off your fees.
  • A license or certificate alone earns the status, but 0% off until insurance is also shown.
  • When a paper expires, the discount ends and verified pauses until you show a current copy. First look and standing-accept pause too. You enter each paper's expiry date when you show it, and Busy Bees texts you a reminder 30 days before it expires.
  • If one paper expires but another is still current, your status stays at whatever the current paper earns. Papers with no expiry date count as current until you replace them. A job you accepted before the expiry keeps the discount it was accepted with.
  • By showing a paper, you confirm it's yours and current. A paper that turns out to be fake ends your paperwork status.
  • Verified unlocks the first look at new jobs, and standing-accept: if you opt in, jobs with a fee under $35 that match your work are accepted for you automatically. It fires when the first look ends, if the job is still there — before the job opens to the rest of the level. Verified pros get their head start to claim jobs by hand first. Verified contractors only, and only if you turn it on. You still pay the fee yourself — nothing is ever charged automatically.

What "verified" means: you showed us your business paperwork — a license, a town business certificate, or insurance — and we keep a copy on file. On customer pages, this shows as "Paperwork shown."
