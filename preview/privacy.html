Privacy — what's counted

Busy Bees counts visits on this site so we can see what's confusing and fix it before the real site launches. The counting is ours: it runs in Busy Bees' own Workspace, not through an outside analytics company, and there are no ad trackers here.

What we count

  • Which pages get opened, and roughly how many separate visits there are.
  • Which buttons and links get tapped.
  • How far a request gets — started, each step, sent, or left part-way — so we can see where people stop.
  • The page a visit ends on.
  • Whether a visit was on a phone, a tablet, or a computer — just the kind, nothing finer.
  • The name of the site that sent you here, if one did — just the name.

Each visit gets a random number that lasts only for that visit. It joins that visit's own steps together (started → … → sent), it's gone when the tab closes, and it's never used to follow anyone from one day to the next. What Busy Bees sees is totals and percentages — there is no screen that shows a single person's visit.

What we never collect

  • Your name, phone number, email, or address.
  • Anything you type into any box on this site.
  • Your IP address, or anything that pinpoints where you are.
  • No advertising cookies, no trackers from other companies, nothing that follows you to other sites.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone outside Busy Bees.

If you're reviewing this site

If you came in through the review gate, your visits on the review pages are counted under your review code — the gate told you your activity is recorded, and the code is how your feedback gets matched to your notes. On the test site itself, visits are counted anonymously.

How long it's kept

The detailed list is kept for 90 days. After that, only the totals stay (how many visits, which pages, where requests stopped) — the day-by-day detail is folded into monthly totals and the rest is deleted.

Your choice

Checking this browser's setting…

The choice lives in this browser on this device — it doesn't need an account and it isn't sent anywhere. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, we honor that too: those visits are never counted at all.
