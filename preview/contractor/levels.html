Levels & verified

Two separate things: your level comes from jobs you've done. Verified comes from documents you choose to show. Neither can be bought — membership never buys ranking, ratings, level, or verified status.

Levels — how the line works

  • Level 1 — 0–4 jobs done
  • Level 2 — 5–19 jobs done
  • Level 3 — 20–49 jobs done
  • Level 4 — 50+ jobs done

New jobs start at the top of the ladder: every contractor at the highest level with a match for the job gets it. Verified contractors at that level get the first look — they're texted first. Inside each group, the higher trust score is texted first, and the texts go out one after another, minutes apart. The clock never runs on a level with no matching contractor. If nobody at a level takes the job, it drops one level at a time — each level's turn has a cap (Level 4 up to 60 minutes, Level 3 up to 45, Level 2 up to 30, Level 1 up to 15) and the clock is shared, so no job ever waits more than 1 hour total before it lands in the Busy Bees dispatch queue. If the top level uses its whole window, lower levels may not see that job at all.

Your level grows with jobs done. Separately, the private ratings customers leave feed your trust score — figured from jobs you complete through the board (70%) and your ratings (30%) — it grows only from completed jobs and ratings, and paying for anything adds nothing to it — which decides who gets texted first inside a level. Your trust score is kept private, and membership can't change it. Declining a job is never a strike against you — a pass is a pass. Not answering counts the same as declining: free, and never a strike.

Verified — show a license and/or insurance

Adding a photo of your license and/or insurance is optional. If you show them:

  • Insurance shown = 5% off your fees.
  • License + insurance = 10% off your fees.
  • License alone = 0% until insurance is also shown.
  • Verified unlocks the first look at new jobs, and standing-accept: if you opt in, jobs with a fee under $35 that match your work are accepted for you automatically. It fires when the first look ends, if the job is still there — before the job opens to the rest of the level. Verified pros get their head start to claim jobs by hand first. Verified contractors only, and only if you turn it on. You still pay the fee yourself — nothing is ever charged automatically.

What "verified" means: you showed us your license and/or insurance paperwork, and we keep a copy on file — that's exactly what the badge stands for.
