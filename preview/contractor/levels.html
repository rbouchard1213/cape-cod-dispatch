Levels & verified

Two separate things: your level comes from jobs you've done. Verified comes from documents you choose to show. Neither can be bought — membership never buys ranking, ratings, level, or verified status.

Levels — how the line works

  • Level 1 — 0–4 jobs done
  • Level 2 — 5–19 jobs done
  • Level 3 — 20–49 jobs done
  • Level 4 — 50+ jobs done

New jobs start at the top of the ladder: every contractor at the highest level sees the job at the same time — verified contractors first. If nobody there takes it, it drops one level at a time. Each level's turn has a cap — Level 4 up to 60 minutes, Level 3 up to 45, Level 2 up to 30, Level 1 up to 15 — and the clock is shared: no job ever waits more than 1 hour total before it lands in Bobby's queue.

Your standing grows with good work and the private ratings customers leave after jobs. Declining a job is never a strike against you — a pass is a pass. Not answering counts the same as declining: free, and never a strike.

Verified — show a license and/or insurance

Adding a photo of your license and/or insurance is optional. If you show them:

  • Insurance shown = 5% off your fees.
  • Licence + insurance = 10% off your fees.
  • Licence alone = 0% until insurance is also shown.
  • Verified unlocks priority first-look at new jobs, and standing-accept: if you opt in, jobs with a fee under $35 that match your work are accepted for you automatically. Verified contractors only, and only if you turn it on. You still pay the fee yourself — nothing is ever charged automatically.

What "verified" means: you showed us your license and/or insurance paperwork, and we keep a copy on file — that's exactly what the badge stands for.
