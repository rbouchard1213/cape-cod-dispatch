Contractor side · Membership

Membership — your own Busy Bees page

Membership is an optional extra for contractors. Board jobs work exactly the same without it — you never need membership to get offers.

What membership gives you

  • Your own page — busybeesdispatch.com/your-company, ready to print on cards, trucks, and flyers.
  • Your own customers request you directly there — you're already chosen, no competing with the board.
  • Own-page bookings: membership plus 10% of the normal fee — a $30 board fee becomes $3 on top of membership — tallied up and billed with your membership, never per job at checkout. The 10% is figured on the normal fee, before any discounts, and figured to the cent — 10% of $25 is $2.50. If you decline an own-page request, it closes, the customer is asked before it can go to the board, and you owe no 10% for that declined request.
  • Board jobs stay the full fee, the same as for everyone. Membership changes nothing there.
  • The Hive, included. Your own customers, estimates customers can approve with one tap, your job schedule, invoices your customers pay through your own payment account, and what each job actually made you — all in one place. Jobs you win on the board land there on their own; you never start from an empty screen.

The price, plainly

  • $19/week · $49/month · $490/year — pick what fits. Membership renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel anytime in two taps; your page stays up until the end of the paid period, and there are no refunds for unused time.
  • Membership never buys ranking, ratings, level, or verified status — ever. Those are earned, not sold.
  • Never required to join the board or accept any job.
