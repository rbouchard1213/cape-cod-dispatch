How it works for contractors

1. Get the text

A matched job arrives by text: the job, the town, the size, and the flat lead fee — all in plain words.

2. Confirm and pay

Reply YES — the text gateway also reads natural replies like “Yeah,” “Accepted,” “Send it,” or a 👍, so a quick answer from a job site still counts. Pay the fee through a secure test checkout, and only then are the job details released. Declining is always free.

3. Do the job

Talk to the customer through the masked relay — neither phone number is ever shown. Quote the job price yourself, on site.

Why contractors like it here

Exclusive leads, pay only when you accept, zero wasted spend. Compare: other boards charge on delivery or per click whether you respond or not. Here, declining costs nothing and a timeout is a pass, never a strike.

Refer a new customer: earn 20% off your next lead when their first request is accepted. Refer a new contractor: you both earn 25% off a lead once they are approved. Codes must be used within 7 days; earned discounts expire after 30 days. You can never use your own referral code — self-referral is blocked: if an enrollment or referral entry uses a code belonging to the same contractor or account, it is rejected. Nothing is ever free — discounts are percentages only.

Levels & trust

Levels rise slowly with completed jobs and your trust score (70% site use + 30% customer ratings — published openly). Levels only add benefits: bigger approve windows, cart discounts, earlier access to recurring plans. Verified contractors unlock benefits sooner. New contractors start neutral and fully eligible.

Proposed — spec review Bands: L1 New 0–4 jobs · L2 Growing 5–19 · L3 Established 20–49 · L4 Trusted 50+. Windows: 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 minutes, never over 1 hour.

For the first core contractors

Founding referral incentive — first core contractors: Among the first 6–10 core roster contractors, bringing another contractor onto the roster earns free membership time: 90 days for the first referral, plus 30 days for each additional one, credited when the new contractor is approved, banked with no cap. During the test run these free days replace your normal 25%-off referral bonus — they never stack. Every core founder also gets a baseline free trial for the test-run period itself, referrals or not. Once the roster reaches 10 contractors the days stop adding up — nothing else ends: banked days stay yours to use and trials run their course. Your founding code is how days get credited — print your code (BUZZ-01 through BUZZ-20, one per tracking slot) on your flyers, and when a recruit joins with your code, the days are credited to you. Codes BUZZ-11 through BUZZ-20 are spare / next-batch tracking codes — they do not extend the founding offer, and free days still stop adding once the roster reaches 10. How tracking works: a recruit's code is captured automatically from the flyer QR ref link or from a “JOIN BUZZ-xx” text, stored on the recruit's record, and only credited when that recruit is approved; manual entry remains the fallback. Self-referral is blocked.

Your own page. Your own customers.

Members get a personal Busy Bees page to hand to customers they already have. The customer opens the link, picks a service, and the request comes straight to that contractor — no phone tag, no starting over. Board-matched jobs work exactly as before; nothing already built changes.

How it works

1. Share your link

Put your page link or QR code on invoices, texts, truck, cards, and social posts. It is yours to share with customers you already know.

2. Customer picks a service

Your page opens with you already picked as their contractor. They choose from the services you offer, answer the same simple questions as the main board, and send one request.

3. You get notified, you confirm

You get the same kind of text as a board lead. It is a request, not an instant booking — you confirm first, then the usual rules apply: fee on accept, then details and the masked relay open.

Membership — the deal

The 10% rule (your pick): a booking that comes through your own page costs membership + 10% of that job's normal lead fee, charged only when you accept it. Declining is still free. No other discount stacks on a member booking — membership, verified, level, welcome, and referral discounts never combine on the same job. Verified and level still give you priority and earlier first look; they just do not take a second cut off the 10%.

What the 10% actually is

The 10% amounts are small, so they are not charged one by one. They add up in your ledger through the month and are billed once, together with the membership — one charge, one card fee, instead of a card fee eating most of every tiny charge.

Member vs. no membership

Same contractor, same jobs — the only difference is where the customer came from. Bring your own customer through your page: 10%. The board finds the customer: full fee, member or not.

Break-even calculator — try your own numbers

Honest check built in: quiet periods are shown too. If your own page brings in fewer jobs than break-even, plain pay-per-accept is cheaper that period — membership is opt-in for a reason.

The fences — what membership can and cannot do

Membership includes

✓ Your own public page with your link and QR code
✓ Your own-page bookings at 10% of the normal fee, on accept only
✓ Straight-to-you routing for requests from your page
✓ The same profile, rating, level, and verified badge on your page

Membership never buys

✗ Ranking or order on the board
✗ Rating, trust score, level, or verified status
✗ Cheaper board-matched jobs — those stay full fee
✗ A saved customer list or open chat — contact still opens only after accept and pay, through the masked relay

Leaving is clean

Cancel anytime on weekly or monthly. Yearly can pause for a season instead of cancelling. If membership ends, your page unpublishes — but your profile, rating, level, verified status, and job history stay exactly as they were, and board jobs keep coming at the normal fee. Nothing is held hostage.

And the no-subscription promise?

The core board promise stands word for word: no subscription to get board jobs, pay only when you accept. Membership is a separate, opt-in extra for contractors who want their own page — it is never required, never the default, and board work never depends on it. That wording still needs owner sign-off before anything goes live.
